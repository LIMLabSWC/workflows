PWM simulator: per-pulse duty cycles + realistic filtering

The PWM carrier is the fast ON/OFF switching. The duty cycle can be generated from a sine/triangle/ramp, or read directly from your editable duty-cycle list. The low-pass filter shows what happens after electrical filtering, motor inertia, LED/eye averaging, etc.

Table mode: the list is repeated as a waveform cycle. Each PWM pulse takes the next duty value from the list. This is easy to understand and good for teaching.
Continuous mode: duty is calculated from a formula for every pulse. This is closer to real PWM synthesis. Smoother output needs a high carrier/signal ratio and an appropriate low-pass cutoff.
